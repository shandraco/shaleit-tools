Tools ShaleIT builds. The free ones are open source and work right in your browser. Nothing you type or upload leaves your device.

About these tools

shaleit.tech is a set of free tools from ShaleIT. They make QR codes and passwords, shrink photos, check text contrast, merge PDFs, convert units and build favicons. They are open source under the MIT License, they need no sign-up, and everything runs in your browser, so the files and text you use are never uploaded.
