Questions people ask

What size should website images be?

As a rule of thumb, make full-width images no more than about 1600 pixels on the longest side, and images inside text around 800 to 1200 pixels. Aim for a file well under 200 KB where the picture allows. The For a website choice here uses 1600 pixels and WebP.

Should I use WebP or JPEG?

Use WebP for websites, because it usually makes a smaller file than JPEG at similar quality and current browsers all open it. Use JPEG for email and anything you send to other people, because it opens almost everywhere. This tool offers both, plus PNG, and lets you change quality.

Does shrinking a photo remove its location?

Usually, yes. Phone photos often carry hidden details such as where and with what camera they were taken. Shrinking a photo here saves a new copy without them. If a photo is already so small that the tool keeps your original, it tells you, and that original still has its details.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The photos are resized and compressed inside your browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded, stored or seen by anyone else, and there is no sign-up. The shrunken copies only exist on your device, and the page forgets them when you close it.
