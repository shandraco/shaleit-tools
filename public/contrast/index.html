    Questions people ask

    What contrast ratio do I need?

    For the WCAG 2.2 level AA standard, normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1. Large text, and parts of the page such as icons and button borders, need at least 3 to 1. The stricter level AAA asks for 7 to 1 on normal text.

    What counts as large text?

    WCAG treats text as large when it is at least 24 pixels (18 points) in regular weight, or at least about 18.7 pixels (14 points) in bold. Anything smaller counts as normal text and needs the higher 4.5 to 1 ratio, so body copy almost always falls into that group.

    What is a contrast ratio?

    A contrast ratio is a number from 1 to 1 to 21 to 1 that compares how bright two colours are. Identical colours score 1 to 1, and black on white scores 21 to 1. The higher the number, the easier the text is to read, especially for people with low vision.

    Is grey text on white good enough?

    Only if the grey is dark enough. The lightest grey that passes 4.5 to 1 on a white background is #767676. Anything lighter, which is common for hint text and captions, fails for normal text. Type your colours in this checker to see the exact ratio.
