PDF merger

Join PDFs into one file, or pull out just the pages you need. Your files stay on your device and are never uploaded.

    Files are joined from top to bottom. Use Move up and Move down to change the order.

    Add at least two PDFs to get started.

    How to use it

    1. Choose the PDFs you want to join, or drop them onto the page.
    2. Put them in order with Move up and Move down.
    3. Turn on Only some pages for any file where you want part of it.
    4. Name the new file and press Merge and download.

    Questions people ask

    Is it safe to merge PDFs online?

    It is safe with this tool, because your PDFs are joined inside your browser and never uploaded to a server. Some online mergers do send files away for processing, which matters for contracts, tax forms or ID. Here, the files stay on your device and no account is needed.

    How do I join PDFs in a different order?

    Add all your files, then use Move up and Move down next to each one to change the order. The files are joined from top to bottom, so the one at the top becomes the first pages of the new PDF. Press Undo if you move something by mistake.

    Can I use only some pages from a PDF?

    Yes. Turn on Only some pages for a file and type the pages you want, such as 1-3, 5. Only those pages go into the merged PDF. This also works with a single file, which is a quick way to pull a few pages out of a long document.

    Do I need to install anything?

    No. The merger runs in your web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no software to install and no sign-up. Choose your files, put them in order, press Merge and download, and the new PDF is saved straight to your device.
